Your idea is actually bigger than a RAG chatbot. What you're describing is an AI Operating System for Knowledge Work—a platform where users connect all of their digital tools, and the AI becomes an intelligent assistant that understands, searches, organises, and acts on their personal data.

Think of it like this:

Google Drive stores files.

Slack stores conversations.

Gmail stores emails.

ChatGPT answers questions.

Your product combines them into one intelligent workspace.

This is a project that demonstrates nearly every major AI engineering skill: connectors, authentication, RAG, vector search, agent workflows, memory, permissions, indexing, and production architecture.

This architecture closely resembles the kind of systems built by enterprise AI platforms such as Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini Workspace, Glean, and Notion AI, but your project can differentiate itself by focusing on user-controlled workspaces, multi-source knowledge retrieval, strong source attribution, and modular connectors. Building even the first three phases well would be a substantial portfolio project that demonstrates production-ready AI engineering skills rather than just chatbot development.

2. Embeddings

Convert information into meaning.

Example:

Text:

"We will deploy payment API next Monday"

becomes:

[0.231, 0.882, 0.124 ...]

Now AI can find related information.

3. RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)

The heart of the project.

Without RAG:

Question
   |
LLM
   |
Answer

Problem:

LLM does not know your private data.

With RAG:

Question
   |
Search your workspace
   |
Find relevant messages/documents
   |
Give context to LLM
   |
Generate answer
4. Source attribution

This is what makes it impressive.

Every chunk should store metadata:

Example:

{
"text":
"Meeting decided to use PostgreSQL",

"source":
"Google Meet",

"date":
"2026-07-27",

"person":
"John",

"url":
"meeting-link"
}

Then your AI can answer:

Source:

📄 Backend Meeting
July 27, 2026
Timestamp: 12:43

💬 Slack #backend-team
John message
10:42 AM
